## What not to compromise on

Everything marked **bold** above sets a gain, a corner frequency or a termination. Taking "something close" there changes the circuit rather than the parts list:

- the two **film** 100 nF are the high-passes in a 61 dB amplifier; ceramics there are microphonic and have a voltage coefficient
- the **1 k / 10 k / 100 k** set the gain and the half-rail reference — if the stockroom has 1 %, take those
- the **49.9 Ω** is the mixer's IF load; without it conversion loss and flatness wander
- a **DIP** TL072: stockrooms usually have them loose, distributors increasingly do not

And the thing actually worth borrowing while you are in the building is a **VNA that reaches 2.5 GHz** — a ~$165 instrument you need for twenty minutes, against which this whole list is rounding error. `docs/radar-hardware.md` covers what to measure with it.
